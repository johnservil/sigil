Help choose Sigil's alphabet: does your device draw these letters?

Sigil writes hashes and keys as short words of letters from several scripts. This page tests whether your device's fonts draw each of the 2,932 candidate letters (41 scripts). It takes a few seconds and runs entirely in your browser.

When it finishes, press Send the result: it opens a prefilled public issue on GitHub, and you press Submit. Or press Copy and paste the result into a new issue or a reply to whoever sent you here. The result holds your browser's identification string (system, browser and their versions) and the code points it could not draw; nothing else. Results from Windows and Android help most.
